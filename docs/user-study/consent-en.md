# Participant information sheet and consent form

## What this is

You are invited to take part in a study of **ScholarPath**, a scholarship
discovery and application platform built as a graduation project at the Faculty
of Graduate Studies for Statistical Research, Cairo University.

We are testing the software. We are not testing you. If something is confusing
or you cannot finish a task, that is a result we need — it tells us the system
is wrong, not that you are.

## What you will be asked to do

One session of about 45 minutes. You will sign in to a test account, carry out
six short tasks (finding a scholarship, checking whether you qualify, uploading
a document, submitting an application, tracking it, and asking the built-in
assistant a question), fill in a ten-question rating sheet, and answer a few
open questions.

We will ask you to say out loud what you are looking for as you work.

## What we record

- How long each task takes and whether you completed it.
- What you say during the session.
- Your answers to the rating sheet and the open questions.
- With your separate permission, a recording of the screen and your voice.

We do **not** record your name, your email address, or any real personal
document. Your responses are stored under a participant code such as `P07`.

## Your rights

- Taking part is entirely voluntary.
- You may stop at any moment, without giving a reason, and nothing you have done
  will be used.
- You may withdraw afterwards by quoting your participant code, up to the point
  at which results are written up. We will tell you that date at the session.
- You may skip any task or any question.

## What happens to the results

Group figures — completion rates, median times, an average usability score — may
appear in an academic paper about ScholarPath. Nothing that could identify you
will appear. Quotations, if used, are anonymous and you will be asked at the
debrief whether you are willing for anything you said to be quoted.

## Risks and benefits

There are no anticipated risks. There is no payment. The benefit is to the
quality of a platform intended for students in your position.
