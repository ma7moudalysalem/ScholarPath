# Screening

Six questions, asked before a session is booked. A respondent who fails any of
the first four is thanked and not booked.

| # | Question | Eligible answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Are you currently a final-year undergraduate or a graduate student? | yes |
| 2 | Have you looked for a scholarship in the last two years, or do you intend to in the next one? | yes |
| 3 | Have you contributed to ScholarPath in any way — design, code, documentation, testing, or data? | **no** |
| 4 | Have you used ScholarPath before today? | **no** |
| 5 | Which do you prefer to work in: English or Arabic? | either — records which task sheet to use |
| 6 | Will you join from a laptop or desktop, a tablet, or a phone? | either — records which layout is being tested |

Questions 5 and 6 do not decide eligibility. They are recorded because the
answers explain differences between participants that would otherwise look like
noise: a participant on a phone is testing a different layout from one on a
laptop, and one working in Arabic is testing a right-to-left interface.

Aim for a mix on both, rather than a sample that is accidentally all one thing.
