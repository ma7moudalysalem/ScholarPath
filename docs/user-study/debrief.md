# Debrief

Seven minutes, after the rating sheet. Open questions, asked in order. Write
answers down verbatim where you can; do not summarize while the participant is
still speaking.

1. Which of those tasks was the most annoying, and what exactly annoyed you?
2. Was there a moment where you were not sure whether the system had done what
   you asked? What happened?
3. When the system told you whether you qualified for that scholarship — did you
   believe it? Why, or why not?
4. When you asked the assistant about travel costs, did you check where its
   answer came from? Would you have acted on it?
5. If this were a real application and this were your only tool, what would you
   still want to do somewhere else?
6. What would you remove?
7. Is there anything you wanted to say that I haven't asked about?

Then:

- Confirm whether the participant is willing to be quoted anonymously.
- Give them the date after which withdrawal is no longer possible.
- Give them the participant code in writing, so they can quote it if they wish
  to withdraw.
- Thank them and stop the recording.
